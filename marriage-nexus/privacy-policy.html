Marriage Nexus Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026 · iPyrrhicTech Studios

This policy explains what information the Marriage Nexus mobile game ("the app", package com.ipyrrhictechstudios.marriage.card.game.nexus) handles. The app is published on Google Play by iPyrrhicTech Studios ("we", "us").

Information stored on your device

The app is played offline, against computer opponents, on your device. Your player name, portrait, coins, statistics, unlocked card backs and tables, your saved game, your house rules and point values, and your settings are saved only on your device. The Maal Calculator's entries are not stored or sent anywhere. We do not run game servers, we do not create accounts, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads) served by Google AdMob. Ads are shown on menu screens and after a finished match — never during a deal. To deliver, personalise, limit and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it,
  • device and app information such as model, OS version and language,
  • ad interactions such as views and taps.

Rewarded ads are always optional. Before you watch one, the app tells you the exact in-game reward (coins, or time with a card back or table). Rewards have no real-world value and cannot be exchanged or withdrawn.

Analytics and crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is played and to fix problems. They may collect:

  • app usage events, such as screens opened, matches and deals started and finished, shows made, and the rule profile chosen,
  • an app instance identifier and your device's advertising ID,
  • device and app information such as model, OS version, language and app version,
  • crash and error reports, including the state of the app when a crash happened.

Your player name is never included. This information is not linked to any account. It is processed by Google under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy. See also Privacy and Security in Firebase and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.
  • Everyone: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your device's Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising, analytics and crash-reporting processing by Google described above, no data leaves your device.

Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, either of which deletes it permanently; your statistics can also be cleared in Stats → Reset stats. Analytics data is kept by Google only for the limited retention period set for the app, and crash reports for 90 days. For data held by Google, use the device controls above or Google's own tools.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

No real-money gambling

Marriage Nexus is a card game for entertainment. Points and coins are a score only; there is no betting, no cash prize and nothing to withdraw.

Security

Information exchanged with Google's ad and Firebase services is sent over encrypted connections.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above shows the latest version.
