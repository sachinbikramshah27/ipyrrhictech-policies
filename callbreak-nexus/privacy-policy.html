Callbreak Nexus Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026 · iPyrrhicTech Studios

This policy explains what information the Callbreak Nexus mobile game ("the app", package com.ipyrrhictech.callbreak.card.game) handles. The app is published on Google Play by iPyrrhicTech Studios ("we", "us"). The same policy is shown inside the app under Settings → Privacy.

Information stored on your device

Callbreak Nexus plays offline. Your player name, avatar, settings, house rules, coins, store items, match history and any saved match are kept only on your device. We do not run servers for the game, we do not create accounts, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob: a small banner, an occasional full-screen ad after a finished match, and videos you choose to watch for a reward. No ad interrupts a hand. To deliver, personalise, limit and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it,
  • device and app information such as model, OS version, language and app version, and diagnostics,
  • ad interactions such as views and taps.

This processing is done by Google under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • EU, EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws: the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.
  • Everyone: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your device's Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising processing by Google described above, no data leaves your device.

Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, either of which deletes it permanently. For data held by Google for advertising, use the device controls above or Google's own tools.

Coins

Coins are earned by playing, a daily bonus and optional videos. They are play money: they cannot be bought, sold, transferred, or exchanged for money or prizes.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security

Information exchanged with Google's ad services is sent over encrypted connections. Game data never leaves your device.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above shows the latest version.
