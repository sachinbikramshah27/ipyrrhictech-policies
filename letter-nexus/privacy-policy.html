Letter Nexus: Word Connect Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026 · iPyrrhicTech Studios

This policy explains what information the Letter Nexus: Word Connect mobile game ("the app") handles. The app is published on Google Play by iPyrrhicTech Studios ("we", "us").

Information stored on your device

Your level progress, daily puzzle streaks, coins, hints, unlocked themes and settings are saved only on your device. Puzzles are generated on the device and the game plays fully offline. We do not run servers for the game, we do not create accounts, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads) served by Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are only shown when you choose to watch one. To deliver, personalise, limit and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it,
  • device and app information such as model, OS version and language,
  • ad interactions such as views and taps.

This processing is done by Google under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Ratings

If you tap "Rate us", the app opens the game's Google Play page. Anything you do there is handled by Google Play.

Your choices

  • EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices.
  • Everyone: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your device's Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising processing by Google described above, no data leaves your device.

Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, either of which deletes it permanently. For data held by Google for advertising, use the device controls above or Google's own tools.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security

Information exchanged with Google's ad services is sent over encrypted connections.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above shows the latest version.
